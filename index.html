ABOUT:

I have spent years, over a decade really, piddling about in small hobby projects. Random word generators of varying complexity, a text sanitizer to en-masse turn a bunch of copied text into a useable array of variables, a simple program to auto-sort computer files into folders by year, month, and chunk folders to keep numbers further down. I also spent some time doing tutorials and little pre-arranged projects.

More recently, I decided I wanted to finally start making actual games, actual finished works of my own, so I have gotten an associates in game creation and am now seeking a career in making games.
